Privacy Policy · Effective November 2026

StitchGrid

iPad Knitting, Cross-Stitch & Crochet Pattern Row Tracker · Developed by Broadmoork Tregory INC

1. 100% On-Device & Offline-First Privacy

StitchGrid is engineered with a strict privacy-first architecture. All user data, documents, scans, settings, and records created inside StitchGrid remain strictly on your Apple device. We do not operate external tracking servers, we do not require account registration, and we never sell or share personal data.

2. Data Collection & Analytics

Broadmoork Tregory INC does not collect, harvest, or transmit personally identifiable information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers (IDFA), or behavioral telemetry from StitchGrid.

3. Device Permissions

If StitchGrid requests access to system capabilities (such as Camera, Photos, Microphone, Notifications, Local Authentication / Face ID, Motion, or Location), those permissions are used exclusively on-device to deliver core features and are never uploaded to third-party servers.

4. In-App Purchases

Optional one-time Pro unlocks and purchase restorations are processed securely by Apple via StoreKit 2. We never see or store your payment card or Apple Account billing details.
