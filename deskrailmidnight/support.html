Frequently Asked Questions

  • What is included for free vs. Full Edition? The free version includes Station 1 (Amber Lighthouse Pier), 2 starter carriages, the first upgrade of each branch, Station 1 diary entries, unlimited Pomodoro sessions, and 2 free ambient tracks (Ocean Rain & Rail Rhythm). A single $9.99 one-time purchase unlocks all 24 ocean stations, 36 carriages, 18 upgrades, 48 diary entries, the full 10-track mixer with 6 mood presets, and iCloud journey sync.
  • Does the app require an account or constant internet? No developer account is required and your core focus voyage saves locally on your Mac. Optional Apple services—iCloud Journey Sync (Full Edition), Game Center achievements/leaderboards, and Apple StoreKit 2 purchase verification—use your Apple Account when online.
  • How do I restore my one-time Full Edition purchase? Open Settings (or any locked station/track prompt) inside DeskRail: Midnight Express and click Restore Purchases while signed into the same Apple Account.
  • Need technical assistance or feedback? Email fmmk336@163.com with your Mac model and macOS version. We typically respond within 24 hours.
