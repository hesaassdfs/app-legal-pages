DeskRail: Midnight Express — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-11-27 · Developer: LANHUM INC · Contact: fmmk336@163.com

DeskRail: Midnight Express (Cozy macOS Desktop Focus Rail Simulation) is designed with a privacy-first architecture. LANHUM INC does not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal data, device identifiers, location data, keystrokes, or third-party analytics.

1. Local Storage & Optional Apple Ecosystem Services

All gameplay simulation, rendering, audio synthesis, local save files, and optional travel postcard exports run directly on your Mac. No third-party tracking SDKs, advertising networks, or developer-operated servers are included.

  • Typing Cadence (Zero Keylogging): When enabled, typing cadence checks only the system idle interval via Apple's sandbox-safe CGEventSource API. It never reads key codes, characters, or text, and requires no Accessibility or Input Monitoring permission.
  • Optional iCloud Journey Sync (Full Edition): If you own the Full Edition and are signed into iCloud, your journey progress (mileage, unlocked stations, carriages, upgrades, and diaries) is synced across your own Macs using Apple's private iCloud Key-Value Storage, governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • Optional Game Center: If you are signed into Game Center on macOS, mileage leaderboards and station achievements are submitted directly to Apple Game Center.

2. In-App Purchases

The optional one-time purchase (DeskRail: Midnight Express — Full Edition, $9.99 non-consumable unlock for all 24 ocean stations, 36 carriages, 18 conductor upgrades, 48 traveler diary entries, 10-track ambience mixer with 6 mood presets, and iCloud journey sync) is processed securely by Apple via StoreKit 2. We never receive or store your payment or billing details.

3. Optional Permissions

If you choose to save an exported travel postcard to your Photos library or filesystem, the app only writes the file you explicitly export.

4. Contact

If you have any privacy questions, please contact LANHUM INC at fmmk336@163.com.
