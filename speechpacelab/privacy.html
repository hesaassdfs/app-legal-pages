SpeechPaceLab — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 · Developer: Broadmoork Tregory INC

SpeechPaceLab (Live Speaking WPM Pace & Volume Coach) is designed with a strict 100% offline, privacy-first architecture.

1. Data Not Collected

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or user-generated content. All calculations, logs, preferences, and files remain strictly on your Apple device.

2. Device Permissions

Any optional device permissions requested (such as Camera, Microphone, Location, or File Access) are processed exclusively on-device in real time and are never uploaded to external servers.

3. In-App Purchases

Optional one-time Pro purchases are processed securely by Apple via StoreKit 2. We never receive or store your payment details.
