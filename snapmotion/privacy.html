Privacy Policy

SnapMotion

Effective Date: November 22, 2026 · Developer: Broadmoork Tregory INC

SnapMotion is developed and published by Broadmoork Tregory INC. We are committed to protecting your privacy with a local-first, zero-tracking architecture.

1. Data Collection & Privacy Commitment

We do not collect, sell, rent, or transmit your personal data to external servers. SnapMotion does not include third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics trackers, or mandatory user accounts. All content, preferences, and records you create remain stored strictly within the local app sandbox (and, where applicable, your on-device App Group container for widgets/extensions) without custom cloud sync or remote developer servers.

2. On-Device Permissions

SnapMotion uses Apple's privacy-preserving photo workflows strictly on your device:

  • System Photo Picker: Lets you select only the specific screenshots you choose to import, without requiring full Photo Library read access.
  • Photo Library (Add-Only) (optional): Used solely to save your rendered 3D mockup images or 60fps MP4 videos to Photos when you explicitly tap Export.

All rendering occurs locally on your device and never leaves your device.

3. In-App Purchases

Optional one-time purchases are processed securely by Apple via StoreKit. Broadmoork Tregory INC never receives or stores your payment card details or Apple ID credentials.

4. Local Storage, System Backups & Exported Copies

This app does not operate custom cloud sync or remote databases. All local records stay on your device; if you have enabled standard Apple device backups (such as iOS/macOS system backups) in your system settings, Apple's operating system may include local app sandbox data in your device backup according to Apple's system rules. When you explicitly export or share files, images, PDFs, or Markdown copies to Photos, Files, or third-party apps via the system share sheet, those exported copies are managed by the destination location you choose. You may delete any record inside the app or remove all local data by uninstalling the app.

5. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app improvements or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data rights, please contact Broadmoork Tregory INC at yaka695@163.com.
