AeroPulse 3D: Sky Circuit — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-11-27 · Developer: LANHUM INC · Contact: fmmk336@163.com

LANHUM INC does not collect personal data through this app. The app contains no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs, and we operate no servers that receive your data.

1. On-device data

Progress, settings, lap records and posters are stored on your device. Deleting the app removes the locally stored data.

2. Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your lap times and career score are sent to Apple's Game Center to show leaderboards, together with your Game Center profile as managed by Apple. Apple's privacy policy applies. You can sign out in your device settings and keep playing.

3. In-app purchases

The one-time unlock is processed by Apple through StoreKit. We never receive your payment details.

4. Photos

If you choose to save a race poster, the app asks for permission to add that image to your photo library. It does not read your photos.

5. Contact

Privacy questions: fmmk336@163.com
