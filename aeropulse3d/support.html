Frequently Asked Questions

  • What is free? 2 circuits, 2 craft and the first 2 career events. One purchase unlocks everything else.
  • Does it work offline? Yes. Leaderboards need Game Center and a connection, and purchasing or restoring needs a connection.
  • My controller disconnected. The race pauses. Reconnect and press Menu to resume.
  • Is progress synced between devices? No. Progress stays on each device. Your purchase can be restored.
  • How do I restore my purchase? Open Settings inside AeroPulse 3D: Sky Circuit and tap Restore Purchases while signed into the same Apple Account.
  • Need technical assistance or feedback? Email fmmk336@163.com with your device model and OS version. We typically respond within 24 hours.

常见问题

  • 哪些内容免费？ 2 条赛道、2 艘战艇和生涯前 2 场。一次购买解锁其余全部内容。
  • 能离线玩吗？ 可以。排行榜需要 Game Center 与网络，购买和恢复购买也需要联网。
