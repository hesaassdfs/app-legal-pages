Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my previous purchase on a new device?
Open GravityChrono, go to Settings or the upgrade screen, and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I request a refund?
All purchases are processed by Apple. You can request a refund directly through reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where is my data stored?
Your data is stored privately on your device (and optionally synced via your personal Apple iCloud account). We never upload your content to third-party servers. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
